# Product Manager — AI/ML Platform (AML / Financial Crime)

## Responsibilities

* **Own the product vision & roadmap**—define, communicate, and continuously evolve the vision and roadmap for an AI/ML-driven AML platform (transaction monitoring, screening, alert triage, investigations), aligning business strategy, regulatory obligations, and measurable client and compliance outcomes.
* **Drive product strategy & market alignment**—assess market trends, competitor offerings, and emerging AI capabilities to shape product positioning; build business cases and secure sponsorship for new investments.
* **Lead discovery & client engagement**—run discovery workshops, user journey mapping, and UX research with Compliance, Operations, and investigator users; translate insights into use cases, requirements, and prioritized product capabilities.
* **Shape AI/ML capabilities**—partner with data science and engineering teams to define, prioritize, and productize ML use cases (anomaly detection, false-positive reduction, risk scoring, entity resolution, network analytics), making informed calls on rules-vs-model-vs-hybrid approaches and the balance of detection effectiveness, explainability, and operational cost.
* **Drive GenAI innovation**—identify and validate opportunities to apply LLMs and agentic AI (investigation copilots, alert summarization, narrative/SAR drafting, knowledge retrieval) with appropriate guardrails and human-in-the-loop design.
* **Build AML domain depth**—partner with Compliance SMEs to interpret regulatory expectations (FATF, EU AMLD, OFAC and local regulators) and translate them into product capabilities, rapidly developing financial-crime expertise on the job.
* **Own go-to-market & adoption**—plan launches end-to-end with Marketing, Legal, and client coverage teams; develop enablement materials, training, release communications, and post-go-live success measures to drive adoption and demonstrable business value.
* **Support pre-sales & growth**—contribute to RFPs, client demos, proposals, and early discovery conversations, acting as the product voice in commercial engagements.
* **Guide Agile delivery**—work with Product Owners, BAs, and dev teams to translate the roadmap into epics and prioritized backlogs, steering sprint outcomes, release scope, and sequencing without losing sight of strategic goals.
* **Measure & optimize outcomes**—define and track product KPIs (alert quality, false-positive rates, investigator productivity, adoption, client satisfaction, model performance) and use them to steer roadmap and investment decisions.
* **Lead stakeholders & teams**—manage senior stakeholders across Compliance, Ops, Technology, and Risk in a global, cross-functional setup; mentor BAs and POs to build a high-performing product organization.

## Technical & Behavioral Competencies

### Mandatory Skills:

* **Proven product management experience**—significant experience as a Product Manager (or senior BA/PO grown into product leadership) owning vision, strategy, roadmap, and outcomes for data-rich or AI-powered enterprise products.
* **Financial services domain experience**—background in one or more relevant areas such as payments, transactions, banking, capital markets, risk, regulatory reporting, or compliance-adjacent domains; comfort operating in regulated environments (deep AML expertise is not required — it can be learned on the job).
* **Strong AI/ML product expertise**—demonstrable experience defining and delivering ML- or AI-powered product capabilities; solid command of the ML lifecycle (data preparation, training, validation, deployment, monitoring) and the ability to engage data science teams credibly on model choices and trade-offs.
* **Applied AI understanding**—working knowledge of core ML techniques (supervised/unsupervised learning, anomaly detection, classification, NLP) plus GenAI/LLM concepts (RAG, prompting, agentic workflows), with judgment on where AI adds value responsibly.
* **Data & analytics fluency**—hands-on ability to work with data (SQL, Excel/BI tools), define product and model KPIs (e.g., precision/recall, false-positive rates), and make evidence-based prioritization decisions.
* **Client & go-to-market orientation**—experience in client discovery, onboarding, enablement, GTM planning, and/or pre-sales support (RFPs, demos, proposals) for enterprise products.
* **Agile product delivery**—strong grasp of Agile/Scrum practices, backlog and epic management, and collaborating with engineering, QA, and design across distributed global teams.
* **Executive-grade communication & stakeholder management**—ability to simplify complex AI and regulatory concepts into compelling narratives for senior leadership, governance forums, clients, and non-technical users alike.

### Nice to have Skills:

* AML/FinCrime domain knowledge—AML typologies, transaction monitoring, sanctions/PEP screening, KYC/CDD, case management, and regulatory frameworks (FATF, EU AMLD, OFAC).
* Exposure to AI/model governance—model risk management, explainability techniques (SHAP/LIME), AI regulation (SR 11-7, EU AI Act), and responsible-AI practices.
* Experience with AML/detection platforms—Actimize, Quantexa, Feedzai, Oracle FCCM, or in-house engines.
* Technical foundations—APIs (Swagger), BPMN/workflow tools (e.g., Camunda), BDD/test automation exposure (Gherkin/Cucumber), or a prior software development background.
* Product or domain certifications (CSPO, SAFe PM/PO, Pragmatic, CAMS, ICA).
