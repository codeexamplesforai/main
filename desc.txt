# Product Owner / Product Manager — AI/ML AML Platform

## Responsibilities

* **Own the product vision & roadmap**—define, communicate, and continuously evolve the multi-year vision and quarterly roadmap for an AI/ML-driven AML platform (transaction monitoring, screening, alert triage, case management), aligning it with business strategy, regulatory obligations, and measurable compliance outcomes.
* **Set regulatory-aligned product strategy**—partner with Compliance SMEs to interpret evolving regulatory expectations (FATF, EU AMLD, OFAC, FinCEN, local regulators) and translate them into prioritized product capabilities, rapidly building AML domain depth to keep the platform ahead of enforcement trends and audit scrutiny.
* **Shape AI/ML capabilities**—partner with data science teams to define, prioritize, and productize ML use cases (anomaly detection, false-positive reduction, risk scoring, entity resolution, network/graph analytics), making informed calls on model selection trade-offs, rules-vs-model-vs-hybrid approaches, and detection effectiveness vs. explainability vs. operational cost.
* **Drive GenAI innovation**—identify and validate opportunities to apply LLMs and agentic AI (investigation copilots, narrative/SAR drafting, alert summarization, knowledge retrieval) with appropriate guardrails, human-in-the-loop design, and hallucination/risk controls.
* **Champion responsible & governed AI**—own product requirements for model governance, explainability, bias testing, auditability, and model risk management, ensuring solutions satisfy Compliance, Model Validation, and regulatory review (e.g., SR 11-7, EU AI Act readiness).
* **Drive discovery & validation**—lead market and user research, feasibility studies, PoCs, and pilots with Ops and Compliance users; use data-driven experimentation to validate hypotheses before scaling, and present outcomes to governance and investment committees.
* **Own the backlog & Agile delivery**—translate the roadmap into epics and user stories (Gherkin/BDD), ruthlessly prioritize using value/risk frameworks (WSJF, RICE), run refinement and sprint ceremonies, and act as the single point of accountability for release scope and sequencing.
* **Measure & optimize outcomes**—define and track product KPIs (alert quality, false-positive rates, SAR conversion, investigator productivity, model performance drift) and use them to steer investment and roadmap decisions.
* **Lead go-to-market & adoption**—own release planning, stakeholder communications, training strategy, and post-go-live success metrics, ensuring smooth transitions, user buy-in, and demonstrable business value.
* **Manage stakeholders & governance**—act as the voice of the product to senior Compliance, Ops, Technology, and Risk leadership; negotiate trade-offs, manage dependencies across teams and vendors, and secure funding through compelling business cases.
* **Mentor & build capability**—coach Business Analysts, POs, and delivery teams on AML domain knowledge, AI/ML product practices, Agile/BDD methods, and regulatory trends to build a high-performing product organization.

## Technical & Behavioral Competencies

### Mandatory Skills:

* **Proven product ownership**—5+ years as a Product Owner/Product Manager (or Lead BA transitioning into product leadership) delivering complex, multi-module enterprise platforms, with demonstrable ownership of vision, roadmap, and outcomes.
* **Financial services domain expertise**—solid experience in banking/financial services (payments, transaction banking, risk, or compliance-adjacent domains), with a strong grasp of financial data, regulatory-driven change, and how enterprise platforms operate in regulated environments.
* **Strong AI/ML product expertise**—demonstrable experience defining and shipping ML-powered product capabilities; solid command of the end-to-end ML lifecycle (data preparation, feature engineering, training, validation, deployment, monitoring, retraining) and the ability to challenge data science teams on model choices and trade-offs.
* **Applied ML understanding**—working knowledge of supervised/unsupervised learning, anomaly detection, classification, entity resolution, NLP, and graph analytics, and the judgment to decide when rules, models, or hybrid approaches best fit a business problem.
* **GenAI & LLM fluency**—familiarity with large language models, RAG architectures, prompt engineering, and agentic workflows, with a view on where GenAI can (and cannot) responsibly augment detection, investigation, and reporting workflows.
* **Model performance & evaluation literacy**—comfort with metrics such as precision/recall, ROC-AUC, false-positive rates, drift, and calibration, and experience turning them into product KPIs and roadmap decisions.
* **Strategic & analytical acumen**—ability to decode business and regulatory needs, quantify opportunity and risk, build business cases, and translate ambiguity into a clear, prioritized product strategy.
* **Data-driven decision making**—hands-on skills to analyze complex datasets (SQL/PL-SQL), define product and model KPIs, and ensure data governance, quality, and lineage across the platform.
* **Technical & MLOps fluency**—strong grasp of modern architecture (databases, APIs/Swagger, web services, event streaming, cloud) plus MLOps concepts (model registries, feature stores, CI/CD for models, monitoring/observability) and how they interconnect for interoperability.
* **Agile delivery leadership**—expertise in backlog management, epic/story decomposition (Gherkin), BDD practices, and orchestrating delivery across data science, engineering, and QA teams (including test automation with Cucumber/Selenium and UAT oversight).
* **Executive-grade communication**—ability to simplify complex AI and regulatory concepts into compelling narratives for C-level stakeholders, governance committees, and non-technical users alike.

### Nice to have Skills:

* AML/FinCrime domain knowledge—AML typologies, transaction monitoring, sanctions/PEP screening, KYC/CDD, case management, and regulatory frameworks (FATF, EU AMLD, OFAC).
* Experience with AML platforms—Actimize (setup/tuning), Quantexa, Feedzai, Oracle FCCM, or in-house detection engines.
* Exposure to model risk management frameworks and AI regulation (SR 11-7, OCC 2011-12, EU AI Act) and explainability techniques (SHAP/LIME).
* Knowledge of BPMN/workflow tools (e.g., Camunda) and experimentation/A-B testing platforms.
* Product certifications (CSPO, SAFe PO/PM, Pragmatic) or AML certifications (CAMS, ICA).
